Privacy Policy

Swastik Plylam House stock and finance app. Last updated: 14 October 2026.

This app is a private business tool used by the owners and staff of Swastik Plylam House (the "Business") to manage stock, dispatch, customer balances, collections and expenses. It is built and maintained by Intellum (the "Developer"). It is not a public app: accounts are created only by the Business owners for their own staff.

What information the app handles

Staff and owner accounts. Each person has a name, a role (owner, manager or staff) and a 6-digit PIN used to sign in. PINs are stored securely by our authentication provider and are never visible to the Developer or to other users.

Business records. Stock items and quantities, sales and dispatch records, customer names, cities, phone numbers and outstanding balances, payments collected, promises to pay, purchases from suppliers, and expenses. This is the Business's own data and is entered by the Business and its staff.

Location (staff only, optional per round). When a staff member or manager presses "Start round" in Today's visits, the app shares their phone's approximate location with the Business owners. Before this happens for the first time, the person is shown a notice and must tap "I agree". Location is shared only while a round is on and the app is open. It stops when the person presses "Stop round". Location is never collected in the background and never when no round is on. Past location points are kept for up to 7 days and then removed.

Assistant messages. If you use the order assistant, the text you type or paste (for example an order message) is sent to Google's Gemini service to turn it into a draft order. The assistant never submits anything on its own; a person always reviews and confirms.

How the information is used

Only to run the Business: showing stock, preparing and recording dispatches, tracking collections and expenses, and letting the owners see where staff are during a visit or collection round. We do not sell personal information, we do not show advertising, and we do not use the data for anything unrelated to the Business.

Who can see it

Access is controlled by role. Buying prices and profit are visible only to owners. Customer balances are visible to staff because staff go on collection visits. Live location is visible only to owners. The Developer does not access the Business's data except to maintain the app at the owners' request.

Where it is stored and who processes it

Data is stored with Supabase (database and authentication). App builds are produced with Expo (EAS). Assistant requests are processed by Google (Gemini). These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms and privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

Business records are kept for as long as the Business uses the app. A staff member's account can be removed by an owner at any time from "Staff and access". To ask for your personal information to be corrected or deleted, contact us at the address below.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and protected by access rules in the database so that each role can only see what it is allowed to see. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect the information.

Children

The app is for business use by adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
